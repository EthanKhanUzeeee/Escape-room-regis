Connect Google (Sheet + Gmail)

  1. Sign in to the event Gmail and create a new Google Sheet (go to sheets.new). Name it, for example "Escape Room Bookings".
  2. In the sheet, open Extensions › Apps Script. Delete what's there, paste the script (click Copy script; your secret key is already in it) and click Save.
  3. Click Deploy › New deployment, pick the type Web app, set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then click Deploy.
  4. Click Authorize access and choose the event Gmail. Google warns that the app isn't verified because it's your own script: click Advanced › Go to … (unsafe), then Allow.
  5. Copy the Web app URL, paste it into Google connector link in the settings above, and save.
  6. Click Test connection, then Sync sheet now, then send yourself a test email.
